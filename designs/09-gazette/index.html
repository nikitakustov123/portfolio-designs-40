The town that went under water and came back

How the people of a village flooded by the Rybinsk Reservoir spent forty years rebuilding their history from photos and letters

Every spring, when the water level drops, old foundations surface. Locals call it “the day the town breathes”. I spent three weeks in Breitovo talking to people who remember streets that no longer exist.

It is a story about memory as labour: a local museum in a former bathhouse, notebooks full of addresses, grandchildren learning lane names from their grandmothers’ stories. It was shortlisted for a journalism award and translated into three languages.
